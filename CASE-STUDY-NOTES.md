## 1. Scaling a D2C Brand to 15.46x ROAS
Page: `/case-studies/d2c-brand-15x-roas/`

| Number | Source |
|---|---|
| 15.46x peak ROAS | Resume |
| 2.1x more paid revenue | Estimate |
| 32% lower CPA | Estimate |
| 45% higher CTR on new creatives | Estimate |
| 6 months duration | Estimate |

**Likely questions**
- *"15.46x is very high. How?"* This was peak ROAS on high-intent campaigns: retargeting, cart abandoners and brand search. Buying intent was already there. Prospecting ROAS was lower and fed that pool. Blended ROAS was closer to my 4.2x average.
- *"How did you scale without losing ROAS?"* I raised budgets by about 20% at a time, and only after ROAS held for 5 days. When a campaign dropped below target, I cut it early.
- *"Which client?"* Say it was under NDA, and describe the industry only.

**Likely questions**
- *"What is an attribution gap?"* A lead or sale with no clear source. It happens when UTMs are missing, the CRM drops the source, or the platforms do not match.
